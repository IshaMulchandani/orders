We are creating a salesman-to-accounts team coordination app for a B2B product company. This app will take orders from salesmen, create those as pending orders in the accounts team, which the accounts team will then MANUALLY, without the assistance of the app, create in a separate software. Once bills have been created by accountants and manually marked as so in the app, the orders will then be passed on to packaging & shipping. Each of these teams should only be able to see the orders that are currently assigned to them. Each team will have multiple accounts. 
Beyond this, however, there will be a 'Partner' role who will be able to see all the orders on a dashboard under the team it is currently assigned to. This role will also have multiple accounts. 
Lets go through all parts of this app:
Order: 
An order is a list of items that one of the clients of the company has ordered from us. This includes:
	a. An order number (automatically generated by the app, 8-digts, starting with 00000001 and going up to 99999999)
	a. Name of the client (chosen from a drop-down which is searchable via keyboard with each entered alphabet narrowing search choices)
	b. Name of the salesman (should be automatically taken as the logged-in user who creates the order)
	c. A list of products (creator of the order should be able to add and number of products between 1 and 50), each with:
		i. Name of product (chosen from a drop-down which is searchable via keyboard with each entered alphabet narrowing search choices)
	       ii. Quantity of that product (manually entered by user creating the order) 
	      iii. Price per piece (manually entered by user creating the order)
   Once an order is created, it is assigned to the accounts team with status 'pending order'. The accounts team will then create a bill for the order in a separate, unrelated software. Once an order is assigned to the accounts team, they may then have access to change the status of the order to 'Bill created'. The order will then be assigned to the packaging & shipping team with status 'Bill created' and then the packaging & shipping team will be authorized to change the status to 'Shipped' once that has been done. After the order status is marked as 'Shipped', it moves back to the accounts department, where the status becomes 'Payment pending'. Once a member of the accounts department manually verifies the payment, they will finally mark the order as 'Done'.
2. Roles:
	a. Partner:
		This role points to the partners who own the company. They have access and view to all functions related to orders. Users with this role will be able to:
		i. Create orders. This includes creating a pending order with a list of items and the order thus sent to the accounts team will have the name of the partner under the 'Salesman' field of the order.
		ii. They may mark the status of the order as bill created, and therefore move it to Packaging & shipping, a function shared with members of the accounts department.
		iii. They may also choose to mark the order as 'Shipped'
		iv. Finally, they may also move the order from 'Payment Pending' to 'Done'
	b. Salesman:
		A salesman is a member of the sales team of the company. They are only authorized to create orders. As described in the understanding of an order, they are to choose from a list of party names (client names, of which, I am able to provide a csv file of, searcheable through keyboard with optimistic key reading). Once an order is created by them, the order will automatically be moved to the accounts team, so, at this stage the salesmen are not allowed to manually change the status of the to anything except 'Canceled'. This is a special capability of the salesmen (obviously shared with partners). After they have created an order and before the accounts team has created a bill, a salesman may cancel an order.
	c. Accountants:
		This is a member of the accounts department. They have 2 key roles in the lifecycle of an order:
			i. They receive 'Pending Orders' from salesmen. At this point, they manually create a bill, outside our app, and change the status to 'Bill created'. Changing the status of the order to 'Bill created', moves the order to the Packaging & shipping department under the same status.
			ii. After any member from the Packaging & Shipping department has marked the order as "Shipped', the order's status is automatically changed to "Payment Pending" and moved to the accounts department. Here, a member from the accounts department will manually verify payment is received, again, from outside this app, and mark the status of the order as 'Done'.
	d. Packaging & shipping:
		These are members of the packaging & shipping team. An order can be moved to them by accountants or partners by assigning the status of the order as 'Bill created'. They will then physically pack the order and ship it via our partners outside of this app. They will then mark the status of the order as shipped. It is important to note that they will mark the order as 'Shipped ', but then the order is immediately moved to the accounts team with the status 'Payment pending'. This change in status is to be done by the app itself.
